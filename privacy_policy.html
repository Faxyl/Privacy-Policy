Privacy Policy — Maldives Harbor Master

Developed by Ahmed Fazeel (Faxyl Podx) • Package ID: mv.fazeel.maleharbormaster
Data Safety Summary: Maldives Harbor Master is an offline-capable single-player arcade harbor control game. We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal user data, device identifiers, location data, or analytics with third parties.

1. Information Collection and Use

Maldives Harbor Master does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). You can play the game without creating an account, signing in, or providing your real name, email address, phone number, or location.

2. Local On-Device Storage (Leaderboard & High Scores)

To save your personal best score and local Top 10 Leaderboard entries, the game uses standard on-device storage (localStorage within the app's sandboxed WebView). This data:

  • Remains strictly on your personal Android device.
  • Is never transmitted over the internet to any external server.
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling the application.

3. Permissions Requested

The Android application requests zero sensitive runtime permissions:

  • No Camera, Microphone, or Contacts access.
  • No Fine or Coarse GPS Location tracking.
  • No Advertising ID or third-party tracking SDKs.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

This application contains no third-party advertising networks, no in-app purchase billing trackers, and no external analytics SDKs. All graphics, sound effects, and Maldivian ocean music are synthesized and rendered locally on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from any user of any age, it complies with child privacy protection standards.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time if new game features are introduced. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
